Auto-Wins 2 cards required

A-01 Auto-win 2 cards · BBB total
Thoracle + Consultation
The cleanest win in cEDH. Two cards, three mana, game over.
Thassa's Oracle
+
Demonic Consultation
How to Execute
  1. Cast Thassa's Oracle. ETB trigger goes on the stack.
  2. In response to the ETB, cast Demonic Consultation naming a card you KNOW isn't in your deck (e.g., "Black Lotus").
  3. Consultation exiles your entire library. Empty library = 0 cards.
  4. Oracle's ETB resolves. "If you have no cards in your library, you win the game."
Glarb interaction Peek the top before casting Consult — if you've already seen the top card, name it. Zero risk of clipping a key piece.
A-02 Auto-win 2 cards · UB
Thoracle + Tainted Pact
Same outcome, different exile mechanic. Insurance against Mindbreak Trap.
Thassa's Oracle
+
Tainted Pact
How to Execute
  1. Cast Thassa's Oracle. ETB on the stack.
  2. In response, cast Tainted Pact. Exile cards from the top until you reveal a card with a name already exiled by Pact.
  3. Since your deck is singleton (no two cards share a name), you'll exile your entire library. Library = 0.
  4. Oracle resolves → win.
Deck Restriction Tainted Pact can fizzle if you have multiple basics of the same name. Your list has only 1 of each basic land variant, so you're safe — but never run more than one of any basic.

True Infinite Loops multi-piece engines

I-01 Infinite Hulk pile · 1 trigger
The Hulk Pile — Body Snatcher Loop into Oracle
Resolves with Oracle on the stack and Dosan locking down counters. The default Hulk line.
Protean Hulk
Body Snatcher
Carrion Feeder
Thassa's Oracle
Dosan the Falling Leaf
How to Execute · Single Hulk trigger
  1. Get Hulk into the graveyard (Entomb → reanimate, OR Culling the Weak sac, OR Natural Order, OR Pattern of Rebirth).
  2. Hulk dies. Trigger goes on stack: search for creatures totaling ≤ 6 mana value.
  3. Fetch Body Snatcher (3) + Carrion Feeder (1) = 4 MV. ETB Snatcher.
  4. Snatcher's ETB triggers — return Hulk from grave. In response, sacrifice Snatcher to Carrion Feeder.
  5. Snatcher's death → exile a creature from your yard (Hulk if needed). Snatcher's ETB resolves — Hulk back to play.
  6. Sacrifice Hulk to Carrion Feeder. Second Hulk death trigger.
  7. Search for Thassa's Oracle (2) + Dosan the Falling Leaf (3) = 5 MV.
  8. Dosan's static = opponents can only cast at sorcery speed → no counters. Oracle ETB → no library check available, but you survive responses. Cast a 0-mana spell to draw out responses if needed.
  9. To actually win: after fetching Oracle/Dosan, cast Demonic Consultation (or have it already up) naming a card not in deck → library = 0 → Oracle wins.
Pro tip If you're pre-Hulk and have Oracle in hand instead, fetch Body Snatcher + Carrion Feeder the first time, then on the SECOND Hulk death fetch Spellseeker-equivalents — but this list runs the simpler line: have Consultation/Pact ready.

I-03 Infinite-ish requires Glarb · non-summoning-sick
Floodcaller Mill-and-Win
Mill your deck via free spells off the top, then close with Oracle.
Glarb
+
Valley Floodcaller
How to Execute
  1. Glarb in play, NOT summoning-sick. Valley Floodcaller in play. You need a sustainable mana source for spells with MV ≥ 4.
  2. Tap Glarb → surveil 2. Look at top of library.
  3. If top card is a non-creature spell with MV ≥ 4 you can pay for: cast it from the top.
  4. Floodcaller triggers (you cast a non-creature) → untaps Glarb (Frog).
  5. Tap Glarb again → surveil 2. Repeat from step 3.
  6. Practical note: not strictly infinite — bottlenecked by mana and by what's on top. Use surveil to dump dead cards into yard, sculpt for spells you can cast. The goal is to mill yourself thin enough to set up a clean Oracle win, OR to keep finding ramp/mana to fuel more casts.
What "win" looks like here You're not looping for damage. You're looping for value: fix your top deck, mill into Oracle/Consult, and end the turn with the auto-win primed.

Value Engines grindy wins over 1-2 turns

E-01 Engine draw → win in 1-2 turns
Necropotence Death Spiral
Pay life, draw 8+ cards, find the auto-win. Often THE turn you win the game.
Necropotence
How to Execute
  1. Resolve Necropotence (BBB). Protect it — losing this loses the game.
  2. End of turn: pay 6-12 life to exile that many cards face down.
  3. Cleanup step → those cards go to your hand.
  4. Untap. With ~10 new cards, you almost certainly have: tutor + Oracle + Consult, or Hulk + reanimator, or Glarb + Floodcaller.
  5. Win the same turn you untap. Hold counter backup with the rest.
Watch out If Notion Thief hits the table while Necro is up, you DRAW NOTHING and your opponent draws everything. Counter Notion Thief on sight.
E-02 Engine free cards every turn
Glarb Top-Deck Engine
Glarb himself. The reason you're playing this deck. A persistent advantage engine that turns every surveil into a real card.
Glarb
How It Works
  1. Glarb in play (3 mana, deathtouch 2/4 — protect with Fierce Guardianship etc.).
  2. Look at top card of library at any time. Free information.
  3. Tap Glarb → surveil 2. Bury cards you don't want, set up cards you do.
  4. Cast lands or 4+ MV spells directly off the top of your library — your library becomes an extension of your hand.
  5. Combine with Vampiric Tutor / Imperial Seal / Mystical Tutor (which all put cards on top): Glarb just casts them next turn for free.
The synergy stack Vampiric Tutor → top → Glarb peeks → cast for free next turn. You're effectively saving 2-3 mana per tutor, every turn. This is why Glarb is broken.
E-03 Engine free cards every spell · taxes opponents
Rhystic Study / Mystic Remora
Tax engines that draw you cards every time opponents do anything. Slow win condition all by themselves.
Rhystic Study
Mystic Remora
When to Cast Each
  1. Mystic Remora — cast turn 1 if possible. Pay cumulative upkeep until it stops being worth it (usually 2-3 turns), then let it die. Often draws 4-6 cards.
  2. Rhystic Study — cast turn 2-3 once you have interaction. Slows the entire table. Either everyone pays 1 every spell (mana tax), or you draw infinite cards.
  3. Both of these extend the game in your favor. The longer Glarb has to grind, the better your odds of finding the auto-win.
E-04 Soft lock 2 cards · synergistic with Glarb
Counterbalance + Glarb Soft Lock
Reveal-on-demand counterspell. Glarb's top peek lets you confirm what'll get countered before opponents commit.
Counterbalance
+
Glarb
How to Use
  1. Counterbalance in play. Glarb in play. You can see top of library at any time.
  2. Opponent casts a spell. Look at the top of your library before deciding to trigger Counterbalance.
  3. If the top card matches the MV of their spell — fire Counterbalance. Counter it.
  4. Use surveil 2 between turns to put a chosen-MV card on top, primed for the next opponent's turn.
  5. This is essentially a recurring counterspell that doesn't cost a card from hand.

Cards that would unlock more lines
  1. Doomsday — assembles a 5-card pile for an instant win. Major cEDH staple, NOT in this list. Adding it gives you a 1-card win condition (with mana).
  2. Bolas's Citadel + Aetherflux Reservoir — alternate "cast everything off the top" wincon with infinite life-drain. NOT in this list.
  3. Witherbloom Apprentice + Chain of Smog — 2-card infinite drain win. NOT in this list (would give you a 3rd auto-win line).
  4. Sensei's Divining Top + Eye of Duskmantle + Aetherflux Reservoir — Floodcaller infinite damage loop. Top is missing from this list.
  5. Hermit Druid — auto-mills your whole deck for Oracle, IF you remove all basics from the deck. The list runs basics so this wouldn't work as-is.
What this means Your deck has a tight, focused win plan — Oracle/Consult as the primary, Hulk pile as backup, Floodcaller as a finisher engine. Three solid lines is plenty. More combos = more fragile to disruption. This list trades raw combo count for redundancy in tutors and protection.
