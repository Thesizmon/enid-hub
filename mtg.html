The Four Win Lines

01
Naked Thoracle

Cast Thassa's Oracle, then Demonic Consultation or Tainted Pact naming a card not in your deck. Library exiles. Oracle's trigger wins on the spot. Glarb's top-of-library peek means you NEVER lose Oracle to Consult.

02
Protean Hulk Pile — The Main Engine

Get Hulk into the graveyard (Entomb / Buried Alive effect / Culling the Weak sac), reanimate it, then kill it with a sac outlet.

Hulk's death triggers fetches Body Snatcher + Carrion Feeder. Snatcher reanimates Hulk → sac again → fetch Thassa's Oracle + Dosan the Falling Leaf. Dosan locks counters. Cast Oracle. Win.

03
Hulk Direct-to-Battlefield

Skip the graveyard step entirely. These tutor Hulk straight into play, triggering the same pile when it dies.

04
Floodcaller Infinite

Valley Floodcaller untaps Glarb whenever you cast a non-creature spell. Loop: tap Glarb (surveil 2) → cast a non-creature off the top → untap → repeat. Mill the deck, then cast Oracle from the top for the win.

Mulligan Guide

Snap-Keeps

  • Land + fast mana + interaction → ideal opener
  • Hand with Necropotence + B source → it's your engine, protect it
  • Glarb + interaction + ramp → grindy but stable
  • Tutor + ritual + protection → can fast-combo

Mulligan

  • No mana acceleration AT ALL
  • No interaction in a known fast pod
  • All 4+ cost cards with no Glarb or ramp
  • Only basics vs. a fast pod that needs T1 plays
  • Two copies of any tutor (redundant)
Tip:  Fast mana = Sol Ring, Mana Vault, Mox Diamond, Chrome Mox, Lotus Petal, Dark Ritual, Cabal Ritual, Elvish Spirit Guide. You want at least one in your opener almost always.

Pilot Reminders

  1. Surveil every turn Glarb is in play. Free filtering is the whole engine.
  2. Look at the top before casting Consult or Pact. Pick a name you just saw.
  3. Glarb is a 2/4 deathtoucher. Hold protection up before casting him.
  4. Counterbalance + Glarb = reveal-on-demand soft lock on chosen CMCs.
  5. Orcish Bowmasters in response to Thrasios / Kinnan card draw triggers.
  6. Sylvan Safekeeper protects Hulk / Glarb from targeted removal.
  7. Talon Gates of Madara exiles a permanent to blank a critical threat.
  8. Necropotence: pay BIG. Drawing 8+ in a turn wins games.
  9. Don't forget Pact of Negation costs WUBRG on your next upkeep — only fire if you'll win that turn.
  10. Cabal Therapy hits twice — cast it, then flash it back for free off a sac.

Cards that would unlock more lines
  1. Doomsday — assembles a 5-card pile for an instant win. Major cEDH staple, NOT in this list. Adding it gives you a 1-card win condition (with mana).
  2. Bolas's Citadel + Aetherflux Reservoir — alternate "cast everything off the top" wincon with infinite life-drain. NOT in this list.
  3. Witherbloom Apprentice + Chain of Smog — 2-card infinite drain win. NOT in this list (would give you a 3rd auto-win line).
  4. Sensei's Divining Top + Eye of Duskmantle + Aetherflux Reservoir — Floodcaller infinite damage loop. Top is missing from this list.
  5. Hermit Druid — auto-mills your whole deck for Oracle, IF you remove all basics from the deck. The list runs basics so this wouldn't work as-is.
What this means Your deck has a tight, focused win plan — Oracle/Consult as the primary, Hulk pile as backup, Floodcaller as a finisher engine. Three solid lines is plenty. More combos = more fragile to disruption. This list trades raw combo count for redundancy in tutors and protection.
