⚙️The Progression Engine
📈
Double Progression
Hit the top of the rep range on every set with solid form → add weight or a harder variation next session, and reset to the bottom of the range.
🪜
Bodyweight Ladders
Push-up: counter → low incline → floor → feet up → weighted. Pull: dead hang → scap pulls → negatives → first chin-up → rep sets.
🛑
The 2-Rep Rule
Can't match last session's numbers two sessions running? You're under-recovered. Audit sleep FIRST, protein second, program never 😌.
🧘
Yoga: Depth, Never Force
Sink slightly deeper each week. Track forward-fold reach weekly — the most satisfying line on the whole chart.
🐾
Never Miss Twice
Miss a day? Fine, life happens. Never miss the same session two weeks running.
😴
Week 13 = Deload
All strength at 2 easy sets, top of rep range. Your body grows during recovery — do not skip it.
🥩Protocol A — Protein (every day, all 120)
🎯
130g / Day
Range 120–140g, spread as 25–40g across 3–4 feedings. One 100g dinner ≠ four 30g meals. Hit it 6+ days/week — rest days included, Sunday is a growth day.
🍳
Anchor Breakfast
30g+ by 10am and the day basically wins itself. 40g total by dinner and you've already lost.
🥤
Shake = Tool, Not Crutch
One scoop/day to plug a gap is smart. Three scoops/day means fix your meals. And don't drink your calories otherwise.
⚖️
Scale Rule
Maintenance to +200 kcal. Weigh 3–4 mornings/week, use the weekly average. Gaining >0.5 lb/week? Trim ~150 kcal. Daily fluctuations are noise.
🐺
Weekly Scorecard
7/7 apex predator · 6/7 the sustainable standard · 5/7 progress slows · ≤4/7 the Day 120 photos will tell on you.
💧
Water
~3L/day, +0.5–1L on conditioning/HIIT days.
🌙Protocol B — Sleep (every day, all 120)
⏰
Tier 1: Fixed Wake Time
Days 1–60. Pick a wake time and defend it — weekends too (±1 hr grace). 7-hour minimum: count backward and that's lights-out. Caffeine curfew: nothing within 8 hrs of bed.
🌙
Tier 2: Wind-Down Ramp
Days 61–120. T-30: screens down, dim room, 5 min box breathing. T-15: light stretching or reading. Cool (65–68°F), dark, phone across the room.
🏋️
Hard Day = Sleep Priority
Lower-body and HIIT days especially — that's when the repair bill is biggest.
🩹
Bad Night Protocol
No naps past 20 min, no caffeine after the cutoff to compensate — just protect the next night. One bad night costs little; two in a row costs a workout.
🔄
Shift-Week Rule
Weird schedule? Keep the wake anchor as close as you can and hit the 7-hour total even if the window moves. Duration consistency beats timing perfection.
📊
Weekly Scorecard
7-hr nights: 6–7/week. Wake-time consistency: 6–7/week. Wind-down (Tier 2 on): 5+/week.
