📋Non-Negotiable Rules
🔥
Always Warm Up
5-10 min light cardio + dynamic stretches before every session. Cold muscles = injury risk.
🎯
Form Over Weight
Perfect your form at lighter weights first. Ego lifting leads to injury, not gains.
📈
Progressive Overload
Add weight or reps each week. Small increases compound into massive progress.
😴
Sleep 7-9 Hours
Muscles grow during rest. Prioritize sleep like you prioritize your workouts.
🥩
Hit Your Protein
Aim for 0.7-1g per pound of bodyweight daily. Protein = building blocks for muscle.
💧
Stay Hydrated
Drink water throughout the day, especially around workouts. Dehydration kills performance.
🛋️
Respect Rest Days
Rest days are growth days. Don't train the same muscle group on back-to-back days.
📓
Track Your Lifts
Log every session. You can't improve what you don't measure. Use this tracker!
